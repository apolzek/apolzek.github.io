My name is **Vinícius Gomes Batista**. I started my career as a .NET backend developer, already on the modern versions of the platform. Since then I have had a strong interest in infrastructure and security, and I naturally ended up moving into reliability engineering, where I spent most of my time.

What motivated me most was the desire for a holistic view, of both the product and the company. I liked understanding the parts and seeing how they connect and interact. That happened naturally and, in the same way, I developed the urge to express the health of systems, their dependencies and their integrations through dashboards, monitoring them and following their evolution. That led me to a second move, into platform engineering, specifically observability platforms, where I spent my last 12 months.

What you will see on this blog is a mix of what I lived through in that time: mostly lessons that cost me dearly and tips about technical work inside large organizations, which often have rigid processes and an anti-productive culture in the name of stability. But you will also see many philosophical texts, inspired by the works I have been reading and by the thoughts and reflections I have been having. That is part of me, and I would like it to live in the same place.

Along the way, if you read my texts, you will end up getting to know me better.

Every post is available in Portuguese and English; use the PT | EN switch at the top of the page. Besides the posts, there are two more things around here:

- [Thoughts](/en/thoughts/): quotes from others and a few thoughts of my own, with dates.
- [Cheatsheet](/en/cheatsheet/): day-to-day commands and tricks, with search and tag filter.
