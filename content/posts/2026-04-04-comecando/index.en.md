*What was your great-great-grandfather's or great-great-grandmother's name?*

I'm reactivating/resetting this blog.. Recently, I went through a small anxiety crisis while reflecting on *memento mori*, that inevitable reminder that our time on Earth is finite. In the middle of those thoughts, I felt the urge to leave behind something that might hold up a little longer against the passing of time: simple accounts of my life, experiences, ideas and thoughts.

That's exactly what I intend to do here: write in an authentic and honest way, no masks, no filters. Write for myself, about whatever comes to mind, without worrying about pleasing or impressing anyone.

Naturally, there will be plenty about my profession as a reliability and platform engineer, my journey with observability and the challenges of dealing with systems in production. But there will also be a lot of philosophy, opinions, reflections, venting and any other subject that sparks my curiosity.

Those who know me know I'm a reserved guy who doesn't like the spotlight. A bit dull and even boring, I admit. But even so, I don't consider my thoughts or my experiences all that boring. And maybe that's exactly why I decided to start sharing them here.

"While we are postponing, life speeds by." — Seneca
